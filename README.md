## Structure of the Submission

### Part A — Diagnosis
Deep diagnosis of three situations:
- **Situation A:** Why the daily alert email produces no action
- **Situation B:** Why root cause fields stay blank (fear, not discipline)
- **Situation C:** Why the 30-minute meeting runs 90 minutes every day

### Part B — Intervention Design
For each situation:
- First conversation and with whom
- Human/process changes in the first 2 weeks (no tools yet)
- Tool design: what the user sees, the workflow, and technology choices
- How to get buy-in from a 15-year shop floor veteran

### Part C — Scale Thinking
- What breaks first when scaling from 2 to 8 CDMO projects
- A system designed at 2 projects that holds at 8
- What to automate vs. what to keep human — and why

---

## Key Insight

> The tools are built for management visibility.  
> They are experienced by the floor as surveillance.  
> Until that flips, adoption fails and data stays blank.
